## About

AI first full stack developer. Claude Code is in the loop for most of what I write, but the calls are mine. Schema design, data structures, and system architecture come from a CS degree I finished cum laude in 2026.

Day to day that is mostly GoHighLevel. Workflows, pipelines, email campaigns, sub-accounts, the LeadConnector API, and the channel side of it: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and wherever else a business already answers its messages.

Then I write the backend underneath. Usually Node, Express, and Postgres or Mongo, with Stripe where the payments are and calendar sync where the bookings are. Five client platforms shipped so far for an automation company in Singapore.

I am early in my career and still learning. Nearly every tool below I picked up on paid client work rather than a tutorial, and I would rather learn a team's existing stack than argue for my own.
